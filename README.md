# Geimori Coffee Research

Open coffee grinding research, burr technology, retention testing, grinder maintenance, and brewing knowledge by [Geimori](https://mywirsh.com).

This repository is a public technical knowledge base. It is **not** a mirror of the Geimori blog. Each article is reorganized and re-written as an independent technical reference, with:

- A clear H1/H2/H3 structure that directly answers a technical question
- Key Takeaways near the top
- Traceable sources — every article links back to its original article on [mywirsh.com](https://mywirsh.com)
- Evidence levels — manufacturer specifications, published research, and community practice are clearly distinguished; unsupported claims are marked instead of invented
- Machine-readable structure — standard Markdown, Markdown tables, and machine-readable data where available

## Content Policy

- We do not fabricate technical specifications, measurement data, test results, user reviews, or citations.
- Performance comparisons state their data source and test conditions.
- Own products and competitors are compared objectively.
- Product model names (GU63, GU64 Gen2, GU38, T38) are used accurately.
- Photos or diagrams from other devices are not presented as Geimori product images.
- Experimental facts, official specifications, third-party opinions, and speculation are clearly distinguished.
- General industry knowledge is not presented as original Geimori research.
- Where evidence is missing, the article is marked **Needs Evidence** and is not automatically approved.
